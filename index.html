Mass performance marketing reached broad audiences, but not the investor profiles that build multi-asset portfolios. The platform needs both a reactivation engine for existing users and an acquisition strategy for new investors who enter with portfolio depth, not one-time curiosity buys.

03 · The Moment
Two products, one window

Collective Invest, your new ISIN-backed bearer bond, removes the expertise barrier for traditional investors. Timeless Investments serves the collector-driven, emotionally engaged buyer. Launching both products coherently, with the right message to the right audience, in Q4 2025, while your fundraise closes, is a compressible window that needs a clear plan now.

ICP Definition & Narrative Architecture

Timeless has three distinct audiences that need entirely different stories to move. First: the existing 80,000 registered investors who need a reactivation hook, a reason to return and build. Second: the Web3-native crypto collector (NFT community members, TCG participants) who already thinks in tokenised assets but hasn't been shown an illiquid long-term play that makes sense for their liquidity profile. We build a narrative framework that speaks to all three without contradiction, and define the right product-to-audience mapping for both Timeless Investments and Collective Invest.
